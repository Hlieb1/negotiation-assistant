# Fuzzy rule base of decision module A. Described in docs/decision.md.
# Syntax: IF <input> IS <term> [AND|OR <input> IS <term> ...] THEN <output> IS <term>[, <output> IS <term> ...]
# Inputs (degrees of negotiation states and confidence, 0..1):
#   anger, tension, doubt, agreement, neutral: low | medium | high
#   confidence: low | high | any (any has a constant membership of 0.2)
# Outputs:
#   calm, argue, clarify, close, pause (activation of a strategy): weak | strong
#   tone: soft | neutral | firm

# Base rule: every strategy has a small activation, the tone is neutral by default
IF confidence IS any THEN calm IS weak, argue IS weak, clarify IS weak, close IS weak, pause IS weak, tone IS neutral

# Anger: de-escalate first, never push for closing
IF anger IS high THEN calm IS strong, tone IS soft
IF anger IS medium AND confidence IS high THEN calm IS strong, tone IS soft
IF anger IS high AND tension IS high THEN pause IS strong
IF anger IS high OR tension IS high THEN close IS weak, argue IS weak

# Tension: reassure, slow down
IF tension IS high THEN calm IS strong, tone IS soft
IF tension IS medium AND anger IS low THEN clarify IS strong
IF tension IS high AND doubt IS high THEN pause IS strong

# Doubt: find out the objection, then give arguments
IF doubt IS high THEN clarify IS strong, tone IS neutral
IF doubt IS medium AND agreement IS low AND tension IS low THEN argue IS strong
IF doubt IS medium AND neutral IS medium THEN argue IS strong
IF doubt IS medium AND tension IS medium THEN clarify IS strong
IF doubt IS high THEN close IS weak

# Agreement: fix the result
IF agreement IS high THEN close IS strong, tone IS firm
IF agreement IS medium AND doubt IS low THEN close IS strong
IF agreement IS medium AND doubt IS medium THEN argue IS strong
IF agreement IS high AND confidence IS high THEN tone IS firm
IF agreement IS high THEN calm IS weak

# Neutral: move the negotiation forward
IF neutral IS high THEN argue IS strong, tone IS neutral
IF neutral IS high AND confidence IS low THEN clarify IS strong
IF anger IS low AND tension IS low AND doubt IS low AND agreement IS low THEN argue IS strong

# Unreliable recognition: ask instead of asserting
IF confidence IS low AND anger IS low AND tension IS low THEN clarify IS strong, tone IS neutral
